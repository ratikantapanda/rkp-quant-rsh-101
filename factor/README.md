# Factor research

This workstream covers factor construction, factor scoring, and research on predictive features.

## Typical projects
- value, size, momentum, quality, and volatility factors
- sector and cross-sectional ranks
- factor decay and stability analysis
- z-score and winsorized factor transforms
- factor model diagnostics
